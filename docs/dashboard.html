The finding

The winding has no wetted area. The housing is closed at both ends, so all 80 W must cross the air-side film on the housing and fins. With Bi ≈ 1e-03 the solid is lumped, and the fin efficiency is 0.995, so the winding, bore and fin tips all sit inside a single 2.8 K band. Only two levers move the answer: wetted area, or air velocity. Thicker walls and better copper do nothing.
